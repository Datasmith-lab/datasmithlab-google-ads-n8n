What happens when you run it again?

The first snapshot saves your starting point. A repeat retains the same keys; an unseen ID is added to the log.

Small scope. Clear limits.

20-ad starter

One domain, US, copy on and details off. Source availability and limits determine what is returned.

One launch per run

The Actor start uses Execute Once. Saved sheet rows never need to launch their own scraper jobs. Avoid overlapping executions.

Your data stays yours

No token, private spreadsheet ID or live customer rows are included. Store credentials in n8n and choose who can access your sheet.

Tested with real delivery

Fresh scraping and real scheduled execution were tested on local n8n 2.26.4 on 11 October 2026. Final read-back found 34 unique saved keys and zero duplicates. This demonstrates the tested path, not full source coverage or future reliability.
